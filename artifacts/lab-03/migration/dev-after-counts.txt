=============== NOTE ON LATER ROWS ===============
This capture is point-in-time: it records the dev database immediately after
the migration and the two seed runs. The Lab 2 Playwright suite was then run
against this same database to prove it is still green, and it creates Tickets
and Attachments of its own, so the live counts are higher than the figures
above. That is Lab 2's normal behaviour and not a change made by this work.

One consequence is worth carrying forward: those specs assign their Tickets to
the LAST active Requester, which used to be siriporn.c and is now the
first-login account, because it is the newest active Requester. The Lab 2 suite
is adapted to log in in a later issue, and that is where the choice of account
belongs; the seed invariant that matters here is untouched - the third active
Requester still owns no Tickets at all.
